# Client Guide: How to Create a Safe Staging Clone in Pressable

> **Audience:** Dr. Jeffrey S. Burns & Practice Administration  
> **Platform:** Pressable Hosting Portal ([my.pressable.com](https://my.pressable.com/))  
> **Purpose:** Create an exact, isolated staging copy of the website to test the new design safely without touching the live website.  
> **Safety Guarantee:** **100% Zero-Risk.** This process simply takes a copy of your site and places it in a private sandbox. Your live website, patient appointments, and daily operations are never interrupted.

---

## 3 Simple Steps to Create Your Staging Clone

### Step 1: Log in to Pressable
1. Open your web browser and go to:  
   👉 **[https://my.pressable.com/](https://my.pressable.com/)**
2. Sign in with your Pressable account email and password.

---

### Step 2: Locate Your Website
1. From the left menu or main dashboard, click on **Sites**.
2. Locate and click on your website: **Jeffrey Burns** (`jeffreyburns.com`).

---

### Step 3: Create the Staging Clone
1. In the top-right corner of the site overview page, look for the **Clone Site** button (or click the **Actions (`...`)** menu and select **Clone Site** / **Create Staging**).
2. A pop-up window will appear asking for a name:
   * **Clone Name:** Enter `jeffreyburns-staging` (or `dreamsmile-staging`).
   * Keep the default option to copy both files and database.
3. Click the blue **Create Clone** (or **Clone Site**) button.
4. **Wait 2 to 3 minutes** while Pressable creates the copy.

---

## That's It!

Once the clone is created:
1. Pressable will show your new staging website in your site list with a private address (for example: `https://jeffreyburns-staging.mystagingwebsite.com`).
2. **You are all done!** Simply let your web team know the staging copy has been created. They will handle all theme installation, testing, and synchronization in that private sandbox.
